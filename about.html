About

A bit of background, and how to get in touch.

I’m a Staff Applied Scientist at Uber in NYC, working on personalization, targeting, and GenAI applications in CRM. Most of my work involves building large-scale recommendation and targeting systems.

I grew up across the Midwest and graduated high school in Northeast Ohio. I received a B.S. in Mathematics from Lafayette College in 2017, an M.A. in Statistics from Boston University in 2018, and a Ph.D. in Statistics from Boston University in 2022, where I worked with Dan Sussman on spectral embedding methods for multiple network data. That dissertation and the work around it are described on the research page.

Outside of work I co-organize a CausalML reading group at Uber, and I enjoy playing soccer, running, reading, and exploring NYC.
