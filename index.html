Welcome

I’m a Staff Applied Scientist at Uber in NYC where I build ML recommendation and targeting systems.

I hold a Ph.D. in Statistics from Boston University, where I studied graph embeddings and inference under Dan Sussman. My dissertation developed spectral embedding methods for multiple network data, and I continue to follow network science and matrix analysis.

Day to day I work in Uber’s Marketing organization on personalization, targeting, and GenAI applications in CRM. I also co-organize a CausalML reading group that brings together scientists and engineers from across the company.

Topics that excite me

Recommendation systems

Large-scale ranking and retrieval, and the messy problem of deciding who should see what.

Causal machine learning

Estimating and ranking treatment effects so models drive decisions, not just predictions.

Graph embeddings & inference

Latent position models, spectral methods, and what you can actually infer from an embedding.
