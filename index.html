I’m a Staff Applied Scientist at Uber in NYC where I build ML recommendation and targeting systems.

I hold a Ph.D. in Statistics from Boston University, where I studied graph embeddings and inference under Dan Sussman and wrote a dissertation on spectral embedding methods for multiple network data. That work, and the talks around it, are collected under research.

Day to day I work on personalization, targeting, and GenAI applications in CRM, and co-organize a CausalML reading group at Uber. Outside of work: soccer, running, and exploring NYC.
